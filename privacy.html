Privacy & cookies

This notice explains how Super5 Malta uses optional analytics on this site.

Google Analytics

This site uses Google Analytics 4 (GA4), a service provided by Google, to understand visits and page views. The GA4 tag is not loaded unless you choose Allow analytics. If you reject analytics, no GA4 measurement tag is loaded.

When allowed, GA4 may process page and session events, the page address and title, browser and device details, approximate location, referring page, and identifiers stored in analytics cookies. Enhanced measurement for scrolls, outbound clicks, and form interactions is disabled. The site does not intentionally send names, email addresses, or lottery picks to GA4.

Google processes this information to provide analytics reports. See how Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and Google's Privacy Policy.

Your choice

Your analytics choice is saved in this browser. You can change it at any time. Rejecting analytics does not affect the site's results, archive, or number tools.

Essential storage

This site stores your analytics choice locally so it can remember whether you allowed or rejected analytics. This preference is used only to apply your choice.
